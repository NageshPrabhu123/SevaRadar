SevaRadar — installable app (works offline)

DEPLOY
1. Go to app.netlify.com > Add new site > Deploy manually.
2. Drag this whole "sevaradar" folder onto the page. Done — you get a link like https://xxxx.netlify.app
   (Or replace the files of your existing site to keep the same link.)

INSTALL
- Android: open the link in Chrome > tap "Install SevaRadar" (or menu ⋮ > Install app).
- Windows / Mac: open in Chrome or Edge > click the install icon in the address bar.
- iPhone: open in Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen.

ANDROID APK (optional)
1. Go to www.pwabuilder.com, paste your deployed link, click Start.
2. Package For Stores > Android > Generate. Download the .zip — it contains the .apk.

OFFLINE SIGN-IN
- Anyone who signed in once with internet on this device can sign in again offline
  (password checked against a protected copy; the real password is never stored). Last 3 accounts kept.
- First-ever sign-in and sign-up still need internet.

PRIVACY (what is stored on the device)
- Only public listings (doctors, medical shops, blood banks, hospitals - public columns only)
  and the logged-in user's OWN data (profile, appointments, chats, prescriptions, notifications).
- Never stored: other users' records, the users table (passwords), admin lists, certificates.
- Sign-out keeps your saved data (for offline sign-in); a DIFFERENT person signing in wipes it.

AUTO-SYNC
- While there is network, everything saved on the device is re-downloaded from the database
  every 5 minutes, when the app is reopened, and as soon as the internet returns.
- New records (new appointments, contacts, hospitals) are picked up every 15 minutes.
- Installed app in Chrome can also refresh in the background while closed (about hourly).
- Offline bar shows when the data was last updated.

EMERGENCY (NO LOGIN)
- Red "Emergency - no login" button on the home page: 108/112/104/mental-health helplines and
  nearest hospitals sorted by distance with one-tap calling. Works offline from the saved list.

WHAT WORKS OFFLINE
- The whole app opens without internet once visited/installed.
- After login, the app saves ALL your data in the background: doctors, medical shops, blood banks,
  emergency hospitals, appointments, prescriptions, notifications, feedback, chat contacts and chat history.
- Emergency numbers and one-tap calling (calls use the phone network, not internet).
- OFFLINE SATELLITE MAP (about 40-50 MB): all of India (overview), your district and town in detail,
  your neighbourhood at street level, and close-ups around saved doctors/shops/blood banks.
  Downloads automatically on Wi-Fi (refreshed monthly or if you move 30+ km), or tap "Offline map"
  on the map to download now. Allow location access for your area to be included.
- AI chat falls back to a basic offline symptom guide (red-flag check + suggested specialist).
APPOINTMENTS OFFLINE
- "Your next appointment" card: date, time, doctor, address, phone — readable with no signal.
- Call and SMS buttons on every appointment (use the mobile network, no internet). SMS is pre-filled.
- Chat messages, bookings, cancellations, ratings made offline are saved and sent automatically
  when the internet returns (pending chat messages show with a clock icon).

NEEDS INTERNET: first sign-in on a device, sign-up, UPI payments, live availability, full AI answers.
