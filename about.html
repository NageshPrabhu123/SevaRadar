📖 Our Story
How it all started

SevaRadar was born from a Design Thinking project at MITE, Moodbidri — driven by one question: "Why is finding a good doctor still so hard in India?"

What started as a prototype idea quickly turned into something bigger — a platform that could actually change how people access healthcare in their communities.

🎯 Our Mission
To make verified healthcare — doctors, medical shops, and blood banks — instantly accessible to every person in India, regardless of where they live, what language they speak, or how much they can afford.
🔭 Our Vision
A future where no one in India has to struggle to find a doctor in an emergency, where every medical need is just a tap away, and where technology bridges the gap between patients and quality healthcare — from cities to the smallest villages.

Built by students. Designed for India. Powered by the belief that healthcare is a right, not a privilege.

What Drives Us
